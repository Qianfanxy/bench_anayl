## 00 · Start Here 设计任务

LEVEL L2 · DESIGN TASK · v2

Foodieland — recipe and food blog website

Design a recipe website: discover featured and popular recipes, open a recipe with its ingredients and directions, browse and read blog articles, and contact the team. Desktop web, 1440 wide, light mode. Work in your own new Figma file — this file is read-only reference.

How to work

1. Build exactly the 5 frames below, named exactly as listed (one state per frame), and wire them as a clickable prototype with the listed links.
2. Everything in “Must include” must appear on that frame. The lists are grouped by subject and sorted alphabetically — they are not in page order and say nothing about layout, grouping, emphasis or styling. Those are your decisions.
3. The two brand colors and the typeface in 01 · Foundations are fixed. Define every other color yourself, plus type scale, spacing, radii, elevation and the page grid, and apply them consistently.
4. 02 · Components gives only a default Button and a default Text Field. Design all other states, the header, the footer, the navigation and every business component as reusable components in your file.
5. Use the wordmark, the social logos, the photos, the category images, the decorations, the avatars and the icons from 04 · Media.
6. Copy and data must match “Fixed data” exactly. This brief wins over anything you may know of the original kit.

Intro: A quick weeknight fried rice that turns leftover rice into a light, healthy meal. Crisp asparagus, soft eggs and a simple soy-sesame sauce come together in one pan in about 30 minutes.
Nutrition note: Values are per serving and approximate.

For main dish: 2 cups cooked jasmine rice, chilled overnight (checked) · 1 bunch asparagus, cut into 2 cm pieces · 2 eggs, beaten · 2 spring onions, thinly sliced · 1 tbsp neutral oil
For the sauce: 2 tbsp soy sauce · 1 tsp sesame oil · 1 tsp mirin

Blog post (04) · header & Q1–Q2

1. Prepare the ingredients — Cut the asparagus, slice the spring onions and beat the eggs. Mix the soy sauce, sesame oil and mirin in a small bowl. [Photo_Cooking_Step] Day-old rice works best: it is drier, so the grains fry separately instead of turning mushy. Break up any clumps before you start.
2. Cook the eggs and asparagus — Heat the oil in a wok over high heat. Scramble the eggs until just set, push them to the side, then stir-fry the asparagus for 2 minutes until bright green and still crisp.
3. Fry the rice and season — Add the rice and toss for 3–4 minutes until hot and lightly toasted. Pour over the sauce, fold in the eggs and spring onions, and serve right away.
