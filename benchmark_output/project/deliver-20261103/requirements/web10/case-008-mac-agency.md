## 00 · Start Here 设计任务

LEVEL L2 · DESIGN TASK · v2

MAC — digital marketing agency landing page

Design the landing page of a digital marketing agency: introduce the agency, prove results, show case studies, answer common questions and invite visitors to get in touch. Desktop web, 1440 wide, light mode. Work in your own new Figma file — this file is read-only reference.

How to work

1. Build exactly the 1 frame below, named exactly as listed. It is a single landing page, so there is no prototype to wire.
2. Everything in “Must include” must appear on that frame. The lists are grouped by subject and sorted alphabetically — they are not in page order and say nothing about layout, grouping, emphasis or styling. Those are your decisions.
3. The two brand colors and the typeface in 01 · Foundations are fixed. Define every other color yourself, plus type scale, spacing, radii, elevation and the page grid, and apply them consistently.
4. 02 · Components gives only a default Button. Design all other states, the header, the footer, the navigation and every business component as reusable components in your file.
5. Use the logo, the social logos and the icons from 04 · Media. The source has no photography: case-study thumbnails, the video poster and the avatars are image placeholders — keep them as placeholders.
6. Copy and data must match “Fixed data” exactly. This brief wins over anything you may know of the original kit.
